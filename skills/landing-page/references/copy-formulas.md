# Landing page copy formulas

Write in the app's language and the owner's voice. Use real specifics: the
city, the price, the number of days, the type of customer. If a fact is
unknown, ask or leave it out; never make it up.

## Headline (h1)

Pick one formula. Aim for 4-10 words.

| Formula                   | Example                                         |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Outcome + timeframe       | "Close your month-end books in one day"         |
| Outcome without the pain  | "Fresh meal plans without the weekly shopping"  |
| For audience, the outcome | "Payroll for Thai SMEs that finishes itself"    |
| Specific number           | "Tax filing for freelancers from ฿990"          |
| Name the job              | "Book a vetted electrician in Chiang Mai today" |

Avoid: vague verbs ("Empower", "Unlock", "Revolutionize"), a single accented
word in the headline, questions as headlines ("Tired of X?"), puns that hide
what the product is.

## Subhead

One or two sentences that answer "how?" or "for whom?".

- Good: "Upload your receipts, we categorize them, and your accountant gets a
  ready-to-file report every month."
- Bad: "Our innovative platform leverages cutting-edge technology to deliver
  value."

## CTA labels

Verb + object, describing what happens on click.

| Good                     | Bad         |
| ------------------------ | ----------- |
| Start 14-day free trial  | Submit      |
| Book a table for tonight | Click here  |
| Get my quote             | Learn more  |
| Download the menu (PDF)  | Get started |

Keep one primary label on the whole page. A secondary CTA uses the outline or
ghost variant and a lower-commitment verb ("See pricing", "Watch a 2-min demo").

## Feature to benefit

Write each feature card as `benefit title` + `how it works, in one sentence`.

- Title: "Never miss a delivery window"
- Text: "Drivers get route updates on their phone the moment an order changes."

Not: title "Real-time sync", text "Our real-time sync feature syncs in real time."

## Section titles (h2)

Benefit statements, not labels: "Everything a small clinic needs to run
bookings" rather than "Features"; "Questions before you book" rather than "FAQ".
Don't put an eyebrow label above every heading.

## Social proof

- Quotes: specific result + name + role/company, with permission. Trim to 1-3
  sentences. "We cut no-shows from 18% to 4% in two months." beats "Great tool!".
- Numbers: one strong fact beats five weak ones. State the unit and period.
- Ratings: include the source and count ("4.8 on Google, 320 reviews").

## FAQ

Answer real objections in 1-3 sentences, plainly:

- Price and billing ("Can I cancel any time?")
- Effort to start ("How long does setup take?")
- Fit ("Does it work for teams under 5?")
- Risk ("What happens to my data if I leave?")
- Logistics (delivery areas, opening hours, languages)

## Microcopy

- Under the CTA: remove risk ("No credit card required", "Free cancellation up to 24h before").
- Form fields: label what you need and why ("Phone, so the driver can reach you").
- Success: say what happens next and when.
- Errors: say how to fix it ("Enter a 10-digit phone number starting with 0").

## Thai-language pages

- Use natural Thai, not word-for-word translation. Keep CTA verbs short:
  "เริ่มทดลองใช้ฟรี", "จองคิวเลย", "ขอใบเสนอราคา".
- Prices with the baht sign and thousands separators: "฿1,290 / เดือน".
- Avoid all-caps and italics (they have no Thai equivalent and look broken in
  mixed text). See the thai-localization skill for typography.
